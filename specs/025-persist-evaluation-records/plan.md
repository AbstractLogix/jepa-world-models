## Design

Notebook 20 uses `(evaluation_seed, start_id, method)` as its unique row key. Its CSV keeps the start value, success, steps, model calls, final position, selected-action error, and off-support fraction. Notebook 21 uses `(group_seed, paired_start, method)` as its key. Its CSV keeps start coordinates, success and status, executed steps, transition calls, runtime, and final coordinates.

The writers assert the planned record count before and after serialization. Reports and canonical project documents link the files and state their row counts and fields. The protocol audit records that the generated CSVs were re-read and checked. These records support inspection and reruns of the toy tutorials; they do not reproduce or validate the cited papers.
